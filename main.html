Goa


Goa is one of the most beautiful holiday destinations in India, famous for its beaches, nightlife, seafood, and Portuguese culture. It attracts tourists from all over the world who come to relax and enjoy the scenic beauty.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach - Famous for water sports and nightlife.
  2. Fort Aguada - A historic Portuguese fort with amazing views.
  3. Dudhsagar Waterfalls - One of the tallest waterfalls in India.
  4. Basilica of Bom Jesus - A UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  5. Anjuna Beach - Known for its flea market and parties.

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry - Traditional spicy seafood dish.
  • Prawn Balchão - A flavorful prawn pickle curry.
  • Bebinca - A famous Goan layered dessert.
  • Chicken Xacuti - Rich curry made with coconut and spices.
  • Vindaloo - Popular spicy Goan curry.
